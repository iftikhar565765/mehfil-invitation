# MEHFIL · محفل — Art Direction
## "The Garnet Darbar" — a garnet-velvet Mughal nikah invitation

### Concept
A royal darbar at twilight. Pearl-ivory pages hung between deep garnet
velvet bands; the hero is the velvet darbar itself — a grand jharokha
arch edged in gold zardozi, brass chandeliers with crystal drops, and a
spotlight vignette like a stage. The visual language is Mughal-Ottoman
court luxury: multifoil arches, zardozi floral sprays, paisley finials,
octagonal darbar seals. Premium transparent PNG art throughout — never
clip-art, never glitter-noise.

### Readability law (the noor lesson)
Raw gold is never body text on light paper; text on ivory uses ink,
ink-soft, garnet or gold-ink. Gold text is reserved for dark velvet
grounds. Ornament and hairlines may use gold anywhere.

### Type
- Display: **Rozha One** — the darbar's carved voice
- Editorial: **Playfair Display** — royal prose
- Script: **Parisienne** — the couple's names
- Arabic: **Amiri** · display **Aref Ruqaa** · UI **Jost** · body **Lora**

### Cinematic grammar
- Garnet velvet letterbox curtains that part on the seal.
- Full-bleed velvet texture on the hero and every dark band.
- Spotlight vignette over the darbar stage.
- Chandelier motes and a slow light sweep on dark-band titles.
- Film grain at 4% multiply across the page.

### Sections
Envelope → velvet hero (jharokha, chandeliers, Ar-Rum 30:21) → story
ivory letter with An-Nisā 4:1 plate → garnet ayat band → save-the-date
→ five Muslim events → palette/particulars → itinerary → venue →
countdown → RSVP → closing dua with the octagonal darbar seal.

### Rules
- Premium PNG art via the white-key flood-fill cutout pipeline.
- Arabic is real webfont text — never AI calligraphy images.
- Garnet stays WARM (red-brown); never purple, never maroon-kitsch.
